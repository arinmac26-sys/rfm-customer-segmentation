# Customer Segmentation Logic

| Segment | Business Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Champions | Recent, frequent and high-value customers |
| Loyal Customers | Recent and consistently active customers |
| Potential Loyalists | Recent customers with developing value |
| At Risk | Previously valuable/frequent customers showing recency decline |
| Cannot Lose Them | High monetary value but poor recent activity |
| Lost Customers | Low recency, frequency and monetary value |
| Needs Attention | Customers requiring further engagement analysis |

> Segment thresholds are implemented as portfolio business rules. They can be tuned according to company-specific CLV, margin, campaign and retention objectives.
