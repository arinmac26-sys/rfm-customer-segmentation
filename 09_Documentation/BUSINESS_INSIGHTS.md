# Business Insights Framework

The RFM model enables the business to move from broad customer reporting to actionable customer-level prioritization.

## Recommended actions

### Champions
Protect retention, loyalty rewards and advocacy programs.

### Loyal Customers
Use cross-sell, upsell and membership benefits.

### Potential Loyalists
Encourage a second/third purchase through targeted offers.

### At Risk
Trigger reactivation campaigns and personalized incentives.

### Cannot Lose Them
Prioritize high-touch retention because monetary value is high.

### Lost Customers
Use low-cost win-back campaigns and suppress expensive outreach when ROI is weak.

## Executive value

The key value of RFM is not simply the score itself. It creates an operational segmentation layer that can feed CRM campaigns, retention programs, customer lifetime value analysis and executive reporting.
