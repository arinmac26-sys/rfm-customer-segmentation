# RFM Methodology

RFM stands for:

### Recency
How recently did the customer purchase?

`Recency = Reference Date - Last Transaction Date`

Lower recency is better.

### Frequency
How often did the customer transact?

`Frequency = Count of Transactions`

Higher frequency is better.

### Monetary
How much revenue did the customer generate?

`Monetary = Sum(TransactionAmount)`

Higher monetary value is better.

### Scoring

Customers are ranked into five groups for R, F and M.

- R: lower days since purchase → higher score
- F: more transactions → higher score
- M: more revenue → higher score

The combined `RFM_Score` is used as the basis for business segmentation.
